# SCP-100 Sample Code of Practice: Reinforced Concrete Detailing

> **FICTIONAL TEST DOCUMENT.** All numerical values below were invented to exercise the RAG pipeline.
> They are NOT taken from any real standard and must never be used for design.

## 1 Scope

1.1 This sample code covers detailing of ordinary reinforced concrete beams, slabs and columns in buildings.

1.2 It does not cover prestressed concrete, marine structures or fire design.

## 2 Materials

### 2.1 Concrete grades

Concrete is designated by grade, for example M20, M25 or M30, where the number is the characteristic compressive strength in N/mm2 at 28 days.

Table 1 Minimum cement content by exposure class

| Exposure class | Minimum cement content (kg/m3) |
|---|---|
| Mild | 300 |
| Moderate | 320 |
| Severe | 340 |
| Very severe | 360 |

### 2.2 Reinforcement

Reinforcing bars shall be high-strength deformed bars with a characteristic yield strength of 500 N/mm2. Plain mild steel bars are permitted only for ties of diameter 8 mm or less.

## 3 Durability

### 3.1 Exposure classes

Exposure is classed as mild, moderate, severe or very severe. Members in contact with seawater spray or aggressive chemicals shall be classed as very severe.

### 3.2 Nominal cover to reinforcement

Nominal cover shall not be less than the values in Table 2. For columns the cover shall also be not less than the larger of the bar diameter and 40 mm.

Table 2 Nominal cover and maximum water-cement ratio

| Exposure class | Nominal cover (mm) | Max water-cement ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Mild | 20 | 0.55 |
| Moderate | 30 | 0.50 |
| Severe | 45 | 0.45 |
| Very severe | 50 | 0.40 |

### 3.3 Curing

Concrete shall be kept continuously moist for at least 7 days when ordinary Portland cement is used. For cements containing blended materials such as fly ash or slag the minimum curing period is 10 days. Members in severe or very severe exposure shall be cured for at least 14 days.

## 4 Detailing of reinforcement

### 4.1 Spacing of bars

The clear horizontal distance between parallel bars shall be not less than the largest of the bar diameter, 20 mm, and the maximum aggregate size plus 5 mm.

### 4.2 Anchorage length

The development length of a tension bar in M25 concrete shall be taken as 40 times the bar diameter. For compression bars the length may be reduced to 30 times the bar diameter.

### 4.3 Lap splices

Lap length for tension bars shall be not less than the anchorage length given in clause 4.2 and in no case less than 300 mm. Laps shall be staggered so that not more than half of the bars in a section are spliced at one location.

### 4.4 Stirrups

Stirrups shall have a diameter of not less than 6 mm. The spacing of vertical stirrups shall not exceed 0.75 times the effective depth of the beam or 300 mm, whichever is smaller.

## 5 Beams

### 5.1 Minimum tension reinforcement

The area of tension reinforcement in a beam shall be not less than 0.12 percent of the gross cross-sectional area.

### 5.2 Deflection control

Deflection is deemed satisfied if the span to effective depth ratio does not exceed 20 for simply supported beams, 26 for continuous beams and 7 for cantilevers.

# Structural Analysis: Course Notes (Beams)

*Generic study notes written for this demo.*

## 1 Standard beam results

### 1.1 Simply supported beam with uniformly distributed load

For span L and load w per unit length, the maximum bending moment at mid-span is wL^2/8 and the maximum shear at the supports is wL/2. The maximum deflection at mid-span is 5wL^4/(384EI).

### 1.2 Cantilever with a point load at the free end

For a point load P at the tip of a cantilever of length L, the maximum bending moment at the fixed support is PL and the tip deflection is PL^3/(3EI).

### 1.3 Fixed-fixed beam with uniformly distributed load

The fixed-end moments are wL^2/12 and the mid-span moment is wL^2/24.

## 2 Moment distribution

### 2.1 Stiffness and carry-over

The member stiffness for a far end fixed is 4EI/L and the carry-over factor is 0.5. For a far end pinned the stiffness is 3EI/L and there is no carry-over.

### 2.2 Distribution factor

The distribution factor of a member at a joint is its stiffness divided by the sum of the stiffnesses of all members meeting at that joint.
